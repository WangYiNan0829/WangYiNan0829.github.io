City Notes: Hong Kong

October 2025

The first time traveling with my father since I grew up.

He waited with me while I took this shot. There were so many people. He didn't understand why I wanted this photo. But he stayed.

Our relationship has always been complicated. This trip was different. This photo reminds me of that.
