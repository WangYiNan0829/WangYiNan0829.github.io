Community Through Photography

Connection, contribution, and memory

Photography has become more than a personal practice—it's how I connect with others, contribute to my school community, and help people preserve their memories.

Photography Club President

As club president, I run weekly sessions where we explore techniques, share ideas, and collaborate on short films and campus photoshoots—building a community that sees photography as both art and storytelling.

Leading the club taught me how to bring people together and make space for creative exploration.

Event Photography

I document other clubs' activities—like the badminton club's competitions, capturing the intensity and joy of the matches. These photos became part of their archive and helped them share their achievements across the school.

It showed me photography can serve others—not just as a record, but as a way to honor people's efforts.

Charity Sale & Instant Portraits

At our school's charity sale, I helped sell club members' prints and set up an instant photo station, taking portraits students could take home right away.

Many later told me those photos helped them hold onto the joy of that day—a reminder that photography isn't just about the image, but about creating memories for others.
